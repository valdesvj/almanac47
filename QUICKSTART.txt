C47_nav - QUICK START (install and run)
=======================================

You need a C47 firmware with ATEXT and GRFNT (the screens write their text with ATEXT) and two
files. FIRMWARE: C47 / R47 firmware 00.109.05.00a0.ALPHA (5 Oct 2026) or later, or one built from
master on or after 30 Sep 2026 (ATEXT came on 29 Sep, GRFNT on 30 Sep). On the older public firmware
00.109.04.00b0 use Almanac47_C47_R47_fw0400b0_v1.1.0.zip from the v1.1.0 release. The release zip has them as .p47, ready to load; from the repository, convert the .txt
files in build/ first (tools/rejig47_atext.py runs rejig and writes the ATEXT / GRFNT steps that
rejig 0.34 does not know; a rejig that knows them converts directly):

  python3 tools/rejig47_atext.py build/NAVFULL.txt build/NAVINIT_FAST.txt

  NAVFULL        the programs that stay on the calculator (all views; it also reads the
                 almanac tables if you load them - TBL_1 / TBL_5, 1 and 5 years, for Free42 / Plus42 on a
                 PC or phone; on the C47 only a short table fits).
  NAVINIT_FAST   builds the matrices, valid 2026-2030 (smaller and faster).
                 NAVINIT_FULL builds the FULL matrices instead (2000-2050, about twice the memory).

v2.2.0 (C47 only): the same views and results as v2.1.0 "Supercharger" (docs/OPTIMIZATIONS.md).
  The views are ALMANAC SPLIT SKY ANIM ALLSKY INFO (NAVTXT is not in v2.x).
  NAVFULL is ONE program: NAV is its only global label, every routine is a local label with its
  name (:SUNA:, :HCZ: ... - NAVFULL_LABELS.txt and the manual say what each one does), so the
  catalogue shows NAV alone. NAV uses the numbered registers R00-R98 and clears R00-R99 (CLREGS)
  when you leave with 0: keep what you need in named variables (NAV does not touch them).
  Other builds are in build/dev/.

1. LOAD THE PROGRAMS (two files: NAVFULL + NAVINIT_FAST)
   Copy both .p47 files to the calculator (USB disk) and load them as you load any
   program file (I/O menu, READP).

2. BUILD THE MATRICES (once)
   XEQ "INIT"          takes a while (it stores a few thousand numbers); it ends with
                       MATRICES READY: FAST 2026-2030.
   The matrices (VL VB VR, NU, ST, ML MB MCL MCB, the planets ...) now stay in memory.

3. DELETE THE INIT PROGRAM (frees memory - the matrices stay)
   NAVINIT is one program, INIT (the matrix builders are numbered labels inside it).
   Delete it: GTO "INIT", then DELP.
   Do not use CLPALL: it would delete NAV and the views as well.
   (Run INIT again only for a new period: load NAVINIT again, XEQ "INIT", delete it again.)

4. RUN
   Only NAV has a name; the other programs show as N01, N02 ... (build/NAVFULL_LABELS.txt
   says which is which). Start everything from NAV:
   XEQ "NAV"  -> NAV asks (once) DATE, UTC, LAT, LON with INPUT; the message line shows the
   formats: DATE YYYY.MMDD  UT HH.MMSS  LAT DD.MMm  LON DDD.MMm  S W -
     DATE  e.g. 2026.0927  in the calculator's CLK date format, as the message line shows it:
                           YYYY.MMDD (Y.MD), DD.MMYYYY (D.MY: 27.092026) or MM.DDYYYY
                           (M.DY: 9.272026); NAV reads it with x→ⅅ ⅅ→J
     UTC   e.g. 18.30
     LAT   e.g. 25.12   (25 deg 12 min N; minus = S)
     LON   e.g. 55.18   (55 deg 18 min E; minus = W)
   (R/S keeps the value shown.) At each prompt the stack is clear and Y shows the format of that
   input only (DATE YYYY.MMDD / UT HH.MMSS / LAT DD.MMm  S - / LON DDD.MMm  W -).
   While the sky is computed a box in the middle of the screen says SINKING....ABOUT
   (also while each view is computed); then the menu on the graphics screen, with the date and time:
     1 ALMANAC  2 SPLIT  3 SKY  4 ANIM  5 ALLSKY  6 INFO  0 END
     KEY A NUMBER   + MENU   ↑↓ ±1 HOUR   9 SNAP      (Free42: 9 PRLCD)
   The menu title: ALMANAC 47, VALID (the period of the matrices, stored by INIT as VAL),
   the date, time and DR position in use.
   Press the number key (no R/S): the item is highlighted and the view is drawn.
   On a drawn view:  +  back to the menu
                     9           SNAP: a screenshot of the screen (Free42: PRLCD);
                                 also on the menu
                     other keys are ignored (R/S stops the program)
                     UP arrow    the same view one hour later
                     DOWN arrow  the same view one hour earlier
   (the hours added stay for the other views; the menu title shows the time in use)
   On the menu itself UP / DOWN change the hour too: the title shows the new time.
   After LON (and after an arrow on the menu) the sky is computed first, then the menu shows:
   the views then only draw. After a number the item is highlighted, the SINKING box shows while
   the view is computed, and the view appears when it is complete (also after + and the arrows).
   (Something lives in NAV at the step after LBL 48. It is 0.
   Try 20 and press + or an arrow ... ;-)
   The sky is kept in matrix ALMC (73 x 4: GHA Dec Hc Zn of Sun, Moon, planets 1-4, stars 1-58)
   and the charts' equator dots in ALMQ (300 x 3, they change only with the position).
   (4 ANIM computes its own times, as before.)
   3 SKY: the name of each body in turn next to its symbol (2 s each, endless);
   (do not use R/S: it stops the program). 0 on the menu ends NAV, gives your registers back and
   clears the screen and the stack. New date or position: XEQ "NAV" again.
     1 ALMANAC  GHA, Dec, Hc, Zn of Sun, Moon, planets, brightest stars; twilight, rise/set,
                meridian passage, Moon
     2 SPLIT    horizon chart on top, the bodies below down to the bottom (8 rows)
     3 SKY      horizon chart, the name of each body in turn next to it (2 s each)
     4 ANIM     the Sun and the Moon moving over 12 hours (24 frames, 1 s each)
     5 ALLSKY   the whole sky: over the horizon above, under the horizon below
     6 INFO     the repository, the licence, no warranty, cross-check with the almanac
     9 SNAP     a screenshot (on the menu and on every view)
   The warning (cross-check with the almanac) is shown on the menu and on INFO only.

   An X in the top right corner: the date is outside the matrices' period (FAST: 2026-2030).
   A body below the horizon (in practice the Sun) has its Hc shown white on black.
   NAV uses the 8-level stack while it runs (the text routines need it) and puts your stack
   size back when you leave with 0. It sets the ATEXT font GRFNT 21 while it runs and
   20 (the standard font) when you leave with 0; after R/S 20 GRFNT puts it back.
   If you stop NAV with R/S or EXIT, the registers R00-R98 keep NAV's values (0 clears them).

PC VERSION (no calculator needed) - python/native/c47pc.py, version 1.5
  python3 python/native/c47pc.py           a window (GTK 3): date, UT, DR, a view, Now UTC
  python3 python/native/c47pc.py --view ALMANAC --date 2026-09-26 --ut 14:57 \
          --lat "25 20 N" --lon "55 12 E" --png almanac.png      (PNG only: Python 3 alone)
  Views: ALMANAC CHART TEXT SKY SPLIT ANIM ALLSKY MOON - the C47 screens of Oct 2026 (header
  line, Moon phase glyph), the same pixels as the calculator programs; MOON (the phases, the
  next new / first quarter / full / last quarter) is on the PC only. --help lists the options.

The user manual (docs/C47_Nav_User_Manual.pdf) shows every view and the map of the
N01, N02 ... labels.

MEMORY: programs (measured with rejig, v2.1.0) NAVFULL 24.2 KB; MOON47 4.8 KB; INIT while loaded
NAVINIT_FAST 28.5 KB, NAVINIT_FULL 57.8 KB; TBL_1 114 KB. Estimates: FAST matrices about 55 KB (FULL about 95 KB), the cache matrix ALMC
about 6 KB (NAV deletes ALMQ, which INIT makes: the charts no longer need it).

MOON47 (the Moon phase on its own, no NAV and no INIT): load build/MOON47 (rejig it to .p47), XEQ "MOON47".
It asks nothing (the clock; if the clock is not on UT: 4 STO "TZ" for UT+4). +/- turns the picture to
the view from the south, any other key ends. Free42: build/free42/MOON47.raw, the same keys.

DOES NOT REPLACE THE NAUTICAL ALMANAC.
