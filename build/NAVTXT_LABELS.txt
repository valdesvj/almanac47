NAVTXT - program labels (NAV keeps its name; load NAVINIT_FAST or NAVINIT_FULL too:
the first NAV runs INIT, flag 81 remembers it; then delete INIT by hand; CF 81 before loading INIT again).
With TBL run (flag 10) and the date inside the table period, the page comes from the tables.

NAV     NAV     text only: asks DATE UTC LAT LON, the almanac page into R50 ..., the stack and the
                lettered registers, then REGS
INIT    INIT    (NAVINIT file) builds the matrices; run by the first NAV, then delete it
N01     ALMR    view 3 TEXT: the almanac page as text in R50-R76 (and the stack and lettered registers); NAV ends in REGS
N02     SDM     text: degrees and minutes "ddd°mm.m'" as a string
N03     SNS     text: latitude with N / S
N04     SEW     text: longitude with E / W
N05     SZN     text: azimuth "ddd.d°"
N06     SHM     text: time "hh:mm" (--:-- when no event)
N07     SF1     text: number with one decimal
N08     SINT    text: whole number
N09     SDAT    text: date "dd-mm-yyyy" from a Julian Date
N10     SUNA    Sun: VSOP87 series (matrices VL VB VR), nutation, aberration -> GHA, Dec, GHA Aries
N11     SUNG    Sun GHA/Dec for the sunrise iterations (tables if loaded, else SUNF)
N12     SUNF    Sun, low-precision formula (0.01 deg) for sunrise/twilight
N13     SER     sum of one series matrix with the matrix functions (COS, DOT)
N14     SERT    the time vectors SV1 SV2 used by SER
N15     NUT     nutation in longitude and obliquity (matrix NU)
N16     STAR    star: SUNA, then STR2
N17     STR2    star GHA/Dec: catalogue ST, precession, aberration, nutation (after SUNA)
N18     SQK     star, quick sin(Hc) from the catalogue (to skip stars below the horizon)
N19     MOON    Moon: SUNA, then MOO2
N20     MOO2    Moon GHA, Dec, HP, SD: Meeus series (matrices ML MB MCL MCB) after SUNA
N21     MOOQ    Moon, quick low-precision formula (BODY list, ANIM)
N22     PLAN    planet: SUNA, then PLN2
N23     PLN2    planet GHA/Dec/SHA/HP: VSOP87 series with light-time, after SUNA
N24     PLN3    planet for the screens: quick position first, full series only if it can be above the horizon
N25     PLNQ    planet, quick position from mean elements (BODY list)
N26     CHZ     sight reduction with all inputs: Dec, GHA, lat, lon -> Hc, Zn
N27     HCZ     sight reduction: Y = Dec, X = GHA -> Hc (R96), Zn (R97), sin Hc ("SHC")
N28     HCZ0    sight reduction for Dec 0 (equator points)
N29     HCZQ    celestial equator: start of the dot-by-dot rotation
N30     HCZR    celestial equator: next point by rotation (no trig)
N31     HCZI    keeps sin/cos of the latitude for HCZ
N32     DHA     inverse: from Hc and Zn back to Dec and GHA (star identification)
N33     RISE    sunrise time UT (SUNRISE program)
N34     SET     sunset time UT
N35     NTWA    nautical twilight, morning, UT
N36     NTWP    nautical twilight, evening, UT
N37     CTWA    civil twilight, morning, UT
N38     CTWP    civil twilight, evening, UT
N39     TRAN    meridian passage of the Sun, UT
N40     PHAS    Moon phase: SUNA, then PHA2
N41     PHA2    Moon phase after SUNA: illumination % and age in days
N42     SBRT    star number by brightness rank (1st brightest ... 58th)
N43     SNMU    star name from its number
N44     CSUN    Sun from the cache (matrix ALMC); computes the whole sky (CALC) only for a new time or place
N45     CMOO    Moon from the cache
N46     CPLN    planet from the cache
N47     CSTR    star R82 from the cache
N48     CSQK    star from the cache: over the horizon test (computes the star the first time: SQK, STR2, HCZ)
N49     CHCZ    Hc Zn from the cache for the body just read (else the real HCZ)
N50     CPHA    Moon phase from the cache
N51     CNTA    nautical twilight am from the cache (the five sun times: computed once per date and place)
N52     CRIS    sunrise from the cache
N53     CTRN    meridian passage from the cache
N54     CSET    sunset from the cache
N55     CNTP    nautical twilight pm from the cache
N56     CALC    computes Sun, Moon, planets (GHA Dec Hc Zn) into matrix ALMC; stars marked to compute when asked
N57     CEQQ    celestial equator of the charts: start (replay from matrix ALMQ, or compute and record)
N58     CEQR    celestial equator of the charts: next dot (from ALMQ, or HCZR and record)
N59     TGET    almanac tables (TBL): Chebyshev lookup (not in NAVFULL_NOTBL)
