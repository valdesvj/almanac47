NAVFULL_TNY - program labels (NAV keeps its name; INIT: NAVINIT_FULL or NAVINIT_FAST)

NAV     NAV     graphic menu (KEY?): asks DATE UTC LAT LON, key 1-7 a view, 8 INFO, 9 SNAP, 0 ends (the only named program; INFO page inside)
N01     ALMF    view 1 ALMANAC: GHA, Dec, Hc, Zn table of Sun, Moon, planets, stars; twilight, rise/set, Moon
N02     HALMV   view 2 CHART: horizon chart left, Hc/Zn of 10 bodies right (no ARIES, no Moon line)
N03     ALMR    view 3 TEXT: the almanac page as text in R50-R76 (and the stack and lettered registers); NAV ends in REGS
N04     HORZ    view 4 SKY: horizon chart, info line per body every 3 s (+ back to the menu, arrows one hour)
N05     HALMH   view 5 SPLIT: horizon chart on top, the bodies below (8 rows)
N06     SDM     text: degrees and minutes "ddd°mm.m'" as a string
N07     SNS     text: latitude with N / S
N08     SEW     text: longitude with E / W
N09     SZN     text: azimuth "ddd.d°"
N10     SHM     text: time "hh:mm" (--:-- when no event)
N11     SF1     text: number with one decimal
N12     SINT    text: whole number
N13     SDAT    text: date "dd-mm-yyyy" from a Julian Date
N14     SUNA    Sun: VSOP87 series (matrices VL VB VR), nutation, aberration -> GHA, Dec, GHA Aries
N15     SUNG    Sun GHA/Dec for the sunrise iterations (tables if loaded, else SUNF)
N16     SUNF    Sun, low-precision formula (0.01 deg) for sunrise/twilight
N17     SER     sum of one series matrix with the matrix functions (COS, DOT)
N18     SERT    the time vectors SV1 SV2 used by SER
N19     NUT     nutation in longitude and obliquity (matrix NU)
N20     STAR    star: SUNA, then STR2
N21     STR2    star GHA/Dec: catalogue ST, precession, aberration, nutation (after SUNA)
N22     SQK     star, quick sin(Hc) from the catalogue (to skip stars below the horizon)
N23     MOON    Moon: SUNA, then MOO2
N24     MOO2    Moon GHA, Dec, HP, SD: Meeus series (matrices ML MB MCL MCB) after SUNA
N25     MOOQ    Moon, quick low-precision formula (BODY list, ANIM)
N26     PLAN    planet: SUNA, then PLN2
N27     PLN2    planet GHA/Dec/SHA/HP: VSOP87 series with light-time, after SUNA
N28     PLN3    planet for the screens: quick position first, full series only if it can be above the horizon
N29     PLNQ    planet, quick position from mean elements (BODY list)
N30     CHZ     sight reduction with all inputs: Dec, GHA, lat, lon -> Hc, Zn
N31     HCZ     sight reduction: Y = Dec, X = GHA -> Hc (R96), Zn (R97), sin Hc ("SHC")
N32     HCZ0    sight reduction for Dec 0 (equator points)
N33     HCZQ    celestial equator: start of the dot-by-dot rotation
N34     HCZR    celestial equator: next point by rotation (no trig)
N35     HCZI    keeps sin/cos of the latitude for HCZ
N36     DHA     inverse: from Hc and Zn back to Dec and GHA (star identification)
N37     RISE    sunrise time UT (SUNRISE program)
N38     SET     sunset time UT
N39     NTWA    nautical twilight, morning, UT
N40     NTWP    nautical twilight, evening, UT
N41     CTWA    civil twilight, morning, UT
N42     CTWP    civil twilight, evening, UT
N43     TRAN    meridian passage of the Sun, UT
N44     PHAS    Moon phase: SUNA, then PHA2
N45     PHA2    Moon phase after SUNA: illumination % and age in days
N46     SBRT    star number by brightness rank (1st brightest ... 58th)
N47     SNMU    star name from its number
N48     TGET    almanac tables (TBL): Chebyshev lookup (not in NAVFULL_NOTBL)
N49     PTXS    TEXT with ATEXT (standard font; Didier's N03 trick) and the number printers - Z row, Y column, X text or number
N50     PINS    FONT big: whole number
N51     PF1S    FONT big: number with one decimal
N52     PHMS    FONT big: hh:mm from hours
N53     PDMS    FONT big: degrees and minutes "ddd mm.m" with sign
N54     PDTS    FONT big: date "dd-mm-yyyy" from a Julian Date
N55     PZNS    FONT big: azimuth "ddd.d"
N56     PTTY    text in the tinyFont (GRFNT 10) - Z row of the base line, Y column, X text
N57     PTNT    whole number in the tinyFont - Z row, Y column, X number
N58     PHLS    FONT big: horizontal line (X = length in pixels)
N59     PSYM    the AGRAPH symbols of the bodies - Z row, Y column, X symbol
N60     HANIM   view 6 ANIM: the Sun and the Moon moving on the whole-sky chart (24 frames)
N61     ALLSKY  view 7 ALLSKY: whole sky, over the horizon above, under the horizon below
N62     WPLS    every view holds its screen here (KEY?): + back to the menu, up / down arrow one hour later / earlier
N63     CSUN    Sun from the cache (matrix ALMC); computes the whole sky (CALC) only for a new time or place
N64     CMOO    Moon from the cache
N65     CPLN    planet from the cache
N66     CSTR    star R82 from the cache
N67     CSQK    star from the cache: over the horizon test (computes the star the first time: SQK, STR2, HCZ)
N68     CHCZ    Hc Zn from the cache for the body just read (else the real HCZ)
N69     CPHA    Moon phase from the cache
N70     CNTA    nautical twilight am from the cache (the five sun times: computed once per date and place)
N71     CRIS    sunrise from the cache
N72     CTRN    meridian passage from the cache
N73     CSET    sunset from the cache
N74     CNTP    nautical twilight pm from the cache
N75     CALC    computes Sun, Moon, planets (GHA Dec Hc Zn) into matrix ALMC; stars marked to compute when asked
N76     CEQQ    celestial equator of the charts: start (replay from matrix ALMQ, or compute and record)
N77     CEQR    celestial equator of the charts: next dot (from ALMQ, or HCZR and record)
