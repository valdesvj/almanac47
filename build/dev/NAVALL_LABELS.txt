NAVALL - program labels (NAV keeps its name; load NAVINIT_FAST or NAVINIT_FULL too:
the first NAV runs INIT, flag 81 remembers it; then delete INIT by hand; CF 81 before loading INIT again)

NAV     NAV     graphic menu (KEY?): asks DATE UTC LAT LON, key 1-7 a view, 8 INFO, 9 SNAP, 0 ends (the only named program; INFO page inside)
INIT    INIT    (NAVINIT file) builds the matrices; run by the first NAV, then delete it
N01     ALMF    view 1 ALMANAC: GHA, Dec, Hc, Zn table of Sun, Moon, planets, stars; twilight, rise/set, Moon
N02     HALMV   view 2 CHART: horizon chart left, Hc/Zn of 10 bodies right (no ARIES, no Moon line)
N03     ALMR    view 3 TEXT: the almanac page as text in R50-R76 (and the stack and lettered registers); NAV ends in REGS
N04     HORZ    view 4 SKY: horizon chart, info line per body every 3 s (+ back to the menu, arrows one hour)
N06     HALMH   view 5 SPLIT: horizon chart on top, the bodies below (8 rows)
N07     SDM     text: degrees and minutes "ddd°mm.m'" as a string
N08     SNS     text: latitude with N / S
N09     SEW     text: longitude with E / W
N10     SZN     text: azimuth "ddd.d°"
N11     SHM     text: time "hh:mm" (--:-- when no event)
N12     SF1     text: number with one decimal
N13     SINT    text: whole number
N14     SDAT    text: date "dd-mm-yyyy" from a Julian Date
N15     SUNA    Sun: VSOP87 series (matrices VL VB VR), nutation, aberration -> GHA, Dec, GHA Aries
N16     SUNG    Sun GHA/Dec for the sunrise iterations (tables if loaded, else SUNF)
N17     SUNF    Sun, low-precision formula (0.01 deg) for sunrise/twilight
N18     SER     sum of one series matrix with the matrix functions (COS, DOT)
N19     SERT    the time vectors SV1 SV2 used by SER
N20     NUT     nutation in longitude and obliquity (matrix NU)
N21     STAR    star: SUNA, then STR2
N22     STR2    star GHA/Dec: catalogue ST, precession, aberration, nutation (after SUNA)
N23     SQK     star, quick sin(Hc) from the catalogue (to skip stars below the horizon)
N24     MOON    Moon: SUNA, then MOO2
N25     MOO2    Moon GHA, Dec, HP, SD: Meeus series (matrices ML MB MCL MCB) after SUNA
N26     MOOQ    Moon, quick low-precision formula (BODY list, ANIM)
N27     PLAN    planet: SUNA, then PLN2
N28     PLN2    planet GHA/Dec/SHA/HP: VSOP87 series with light-time, after SUNA
N29     PLN3    planet for the screens: quick position first, full series only if it can be above the horizon
N30     PLNQ    planet, quick position from mean elements (BODY list)
N31     CHZ     sight reduction with all inputs: Dec, GHA, lat, lon -> Hc, Zn
N32     HCZ     sight reduction: Y = Dec, X = GHA -> Hc (R96), Zn (R97), sin Hc ("SHC")
N33     HCZ0    sight reduction for Dec 0 (equator points)
N34     HCZQ    celestial equator: start of the dot-by-dot rotation
N35     HCZR    celestial equator: next point by rotation (no trig)
N36     HCZI    keeps sin/cos of the latitude for HCZ
N37     DHA     inverse: from Hc and Zn back to Dec and GHA (star identification)
N38     RISE    sunrise time UT (SUNRISE program)
N39     SET     sunset time UT
N40     NTWA    nautical twilight, morning, UT
N41     NTWP    nautical twilight, evening, UT
N42     CTWA    civil twilight, morning, UT
N43     CTWP    civil twilight, evening, UT
N44     TRAN    meridian passage of the Sun, UT
N45     PHAS    Moon phase: SUNA, then PHA2
N46     PHA2    Moon phase after SUNA: illumination % and age in days
N47     SBRT    star number by brightness rank (1st brightest ... 58th)
N48     SNMU    star name from its number
N49     PTXS    FONT big (C47 status-bar font, 12 px, AGRAPH): draw a string - Z row, Y column, X text
N50     PINS    FONT big: whole number
N51     PF1S    FONT big: number with one decimal
N52     PHMS    FONT big: hh:mm from hours
N53     PDMS    FONT big: degrees and minutes "ddd mm.m" with sign
N54     PDTS    FONT big: date "dd-mm-yyyy" from a Julian Date
N55     PZNS    FONT big: azimuth "ddd.d"
N56     PHLS    FONT big: horizontal line (X = length in pixels)
N57     PTXT    FONT small (3x5, AGRAPH): draw a string - Z row, Y column, X text
N58     PTNS    FONT small: whole number
N59     PT1     FONT small: number with one decimal
N60     HANIM   view 6 ANIM: the Sun and the Moon moving on the whole-sky chart (24 frames)
N61     ALLSKY  view 7 ALLSKY: whole sky, over the horizon above, under the horizon below
N62     WPLS    every view holds its screen here (KEY?): + back to the menu, up / down arrow one hour later / earlier
N63     CSUN    Sun from the cache (matrix ALMC); computes the whole sky (CALC) only for a new time or place
N64     CMOO    Moon from the cache
N65     CPLN    planet from the cache
N66     CSTR    star R82 from the cache
N67     CSQK    star from the cache: over the horizon test (computes the star the first time: SQK, STR2, HCZ)
N68     CHCZ    Hc Zn from the cache for the body just read (else the real HCZ)
N69     CPHA    Moon phase from the cache
N70     CNTA    nautical twilight am from the cache (the five sun times: computed once per date and place)
N71     CRIS    sunrise from the cache
N72     CTRN    meridian passage from the cache
N73     CSET    sunset from the cache
N74     CNTP    nautical twilight pm from the cache
N75     CALC    computes Sun, Moon, planets (GHA Dec Hc Zn) into matrix ALMC; stars marked to compute when asked
N76     CEQQ    celestial equator of the charts: start (replay from matrix ALMQ, or compute and record)
N77     CEQR    celestial equator of the charts: next dot (from ALMQ, or HCZR and record)
