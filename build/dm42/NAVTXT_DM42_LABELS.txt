NAVTXT_DM42 - program labels (NAV keeps its name; INIT is NAVINIT_DM42)

NAV     NAV     graphic menu (KEY?): asks DATE UTC LAT LON, key 1-8 a view, 9 INFO, 0 ends (the only named program; INFO page inside)
N01     ALMR    view 3 TEXT: the almanac page as text in R50-R76 (and the stack and lettered registers); NAV ends in REGS
N02     SDM     text: degrees and minutes "ddd°mm.m'" as a string
N03     SNS     text: latitude with N / S
N04     SEW     text: longitude with E / W
N05     SZN     text: azimuth "ddd.d°"
N06     SHM     text: time "hh:mm" (--:-- when no event)
N07     SF1     text: number with one decimal
N08     SINT    text: whole number
N09     SDAT    text: date "dd-mm-yyyy" from a Julian Date
N10     SUNA    Sun: VSOP87 series (matrices VL VB VR), nutation, aberration -> GHA, Dec, GHA Aries
N11     SUNG    Sun GHA/Dec for the sunrise iterations (tables if loaded, else SUNF)
N12     SUNF    Sun, low-precision formula (0.01 deg) for sunrise/twilight
N13     SER     sum of one series matrix with the matrix functions (COS, DOT)
N14     SERT    the time vectors SV1 SV2 used by SER
N15     NUT     nutation in longitude and obliquity (matrix NU)
N16     STAR    star: SUNA, then STR2
N17     STR2    star GHA/Dec: catalogue ST, precession, aberration, nutation (after SUNA)
N18     SQK     star, quick sin(Hc) from the catalogue (to skip stars below the horizon)
N19     CHZ     sight reduction with all inputs: Dec, GHA, lat, lon -> Hc, Zn
N20     HCZ     sight reduction: Y = Dec, X = GHA -> Hc (R96), Zn (R97), sin Hc ("SHC")
N21     HCZ0    sight reduction for Dec 0 (equator points)
N22     HCZQ    celestial equator: start of the dot-by-dot rotation
N23     HCZR    celestial equator: next point by rotation (no trig)
N24     HCZI    keeps sin/cos of the latitude for HCZ
N25     DHA     inverse: from Hc and Zn back to Dec and GHA (star identification)
N26     RISE    sunrise time UT (SUNRISE program)
N27     SET     sunset time UT
N28     NTWA    nautical twilight, morning, UT
N29     NTWP    nautical twilight, evening, UT
N30     CTWA    civil twilight, morning, UT
N31     CTWP    civil twilight, evening, UT
N32     TRAN    meridian passage of the Sun, UT
N33     SBRT    star number by brightness rank (1st brightest ... 58th)
N34     SNMU    star name from its number
N35     CSUN    Sun from the cache (matrix ALMC); computes the whole sky (CALC) only for a new time or place
N36     CMOO    Moon from the cache
N37     CPLN    planet from the cache
N38     CSTR    star R82 from the cache
N39     CSQK    star from the cache: over the horizon test (computes the star the first time: SQK, STR2, HCZ)
N40     CHCZ    Hc Zn from the cache for the body just read (else the real HCZ)
N41     CPHA    Moon phase from the cache
N42     CNTA    nautical twilight am from the cache (the five sun times: computed once per date and place)
N43     CRIS    sunrise from the cache
N44     CTRN    meridian passage from the cache
N45     CSET    sunset from the cache
N46     CNTP    nautical twilight pm from the cache
N47     CALC    computes Sun, Moon, planets (GHA Dec Hc Zn) into matrix ALMC; stars marked to compute when asked
N48     CEQQ    celestial equator of the charts: start (replay from matrix ALMQ, or compute and record)
N49     CEQR    celestial equator of the charts: next dot (from ALMQ, or HCZR and record)
