NAV12_DM42 - program labels (NAV keeps its name; INIT is NAVINIT_LITTLE)

NAV     NAV     graphic menu (KEY?): asks DATE UTC LAT LON, key 1-7 a view, 8 INFO, 9 SNAP, 0 ends (the only named program; INFO page inside)
N01     ALMF    view 1 ALMANAC: GHA, Dec, Hc, Zn table of Sun, Moon, planets, stars; twilight, rise/set, Moon
N02     HALMV   view 2 CHART: horizon chart left, Hc/Zn of 10 bodies right (no ARIES, no Moon line)
N03     ALMR    view 3 TEXT: the almanac page as text in R50-R76 (and the stack and lettered registers); NAV ends in REGS
N04     SDM     text: degrees and minutes "ddd°mm.m'" as a string
N05     SNS     text: latitude with N / S
N06     SEW     text: longitude with E / W
N07     SZN     text: azimuth "ddd.d°"
N08     SHM     text: time "hh:mm" (--:-- when no event)
N09     SF1     text: number with one decimal
N10     SINT    text: whole number
N11     SDAT    text: date "dd-mm-yyyy" from a Julian Date
N12     SUNA    Sun: VSOP87 series (matrices VL VB VR), nutation, aberration -> GHA, Dec, GHA Aries
N13     SUNG    Sun GHA/Dec for the sunrise iterations (tables if loaded, else SUNF)
N14     SUNF    Sun, low-precision formula (0.01 deg) for sunrise/twilight
N15     SER     sum of one series matrix with the matrix functions (COS, DOT)
N16     SERT    the time vectors SV1 SV2 used by SER
N17     NUT     nutation in longitude and obliquity (matrix NU)
N18     STAR    star: SUNA, then STR2
N19     STR2    star GHA/Dec: catalogue ST, precession, aberration, nutation (after SUNA)
N20     SQK     star, quick sin(Hc) from the catalogue (to skip stars below the horizon)
N21     CHZ     sight reduction with all inputs: Dec, GHA, lat, lon -> Hc, Zn
N22     HCZ     sight reduction: Y = Dec, X = GHA -> Hc (R96), Zn (R97), sin Hc ("SHC")
N23     HCZ0    sight reduction for Dec 0 (equator points)
N24     HCZQ    celestial equator: start of the dot-by-dot rotation
N25     HCZR    celestial equator: next point by rotation (no trig)
N26     HCZI    keeps sin/cos of the latitude for HCZ
N27     DHA     inverse: from Hc and Zn back to Dec and GHA (star identification)
N28     RISE    sunrise time UT (SUNRISE program)
N29     SET     sunset time UT
N30     NTWA    nautical twilight, morning, UT
N31     NTWP    nautical twilight, evening, UT
N32     CTWA    civil twilight, morning, UT
N33     CTWP    civil twilight, evening, UT
N34     TRAN    meridian passage of the Sun, UT
N35     SBRT    star number by brightness rank (1st brightest ... 58th)
N36     SNMU    star name from its number
N37     PTXS    TEXT: ATEXT in GRFNT 21 (Didier's N03 trick) - Z row of the base line, Y column, X text
N38     PINS    TEXT: whole number (one ATEXT)
N39     PF1S    TEXT: number with one decimal
N40     PHMS    TEXT: hh:mm from hours
N41     PDMS    TEXT: degrees and minutes "ddd mm.m" with sign
N42     PDTS    TEXT: date "dd-mm-yyyy" from a Julian Date
N43     PZNS    TEXT: azimuth "ddd.d"
N44     PHLS    horizontal line (X = length in pixels)
N48     WPLS    every view holds its screen here (KEY?): + back to the menu, up / down arrow one hour later / earlier
N49     TGET    almanac tables (TBL): Chebyshev lookup (not in NAVFULL_NOTBL)
N50     PTTY    TEXT in the tinyFont (GRFNT 10, the charts) - Z row of the base line, Y column, X text
N51     PTNT    TEXT: whole number in the tinyFont
N52     PSYB    SYMBOL of a body, 12 rows (glyphs47, AGRAPH; the tables, the Moon phases) - Z row, Y column, X symbol
N53     PSYS    SYMBOL of a body, 7 rows (glyphs47, AGRAPH; the charts) - Z row, Y column, X symbol
