NAVTXT_DM42 - program labels (NAV keeps its name; INIT is NAVINIT_LITTLE)

NAV     NAV     graphic menu (KEY?): asks DATE UTC LAT LON, key 1-8 a view, 9 INFO, 0 ends (the only named program; INFO page inside)
N01     ALMR    view 3 TEXT: the almanac page as text in R50-R76 (and the stack and lettered registers); NAV ends in REGS
N02     SDM     text: degrees and minutes "ddd°mm.m'" as a string
N03     SNS     text: latitude with N / S
N04     SEW     text: longitude with E / W
N05     SZN     text: azimuth "ddd.d°"
N06     SHM     text: time "hh:mm" (--:-- when no event)
N07     SF1     text: number with one decimal
N08     SINT    text: whole number
N09     SDAT    text: date "dd-mm-yyyy" from a Julian Date
N10     SUNA    Sun: VSOP87 series (matrices VL VB VR), nutation, aberration -> GHA, Dec, GHA Aries
N11     SUNG    Sun GHA/Dec for the sunrise iterations (tables if loaded, else SUNF)
N12     SUNF    Sun, low-precision formula (0.01 deg) for sunrise/twilight
N13     SER     sum of one series matrix with the matrix functions (COS, DOT)
N14     SERT    the time vectors SV1 SV2 used by SER
N15     NUT     nutation in longitude and obliquity (matrix NU)
N16     STAR    star: SUNA, then STR2
N17     STR2    star GHA/Dec: catalogue ST, precession, aberration, nutation (after SUNA)
N18     SQK     star, quick sin(Hc) from the catalogue (to skip stars below the horizon)
N19     CHZ     sight reduction with all inputs: Dec, GHA, lat, lon -> Hc, Zn
N20     HCZ     sight reduction: Y = Dec, X = GHA -> Hc (R96), Zn (R97), sin Hc ("SHC")
N21     HCZ0    sight reduction for Dec 0 (equator points)
N22     HCZI    keeps sin/cos of the latitude for HCZ
N23     DHA     inverse: from Hc and Zn back to Dec and GHA (star identification)
N24     RISE    sunrise time UT (SUNRISE program)
N25     SET     sunset time UT
N26     NTWA    nautical twilight, morning, UT
N27     NTWP    nautical twilight, evening, UT
N28     CTWA    civil twilight, morning, UT
N29     CTWP    civil twilight, evening, UT
N30     TRAN    meridian passage of the Sun, UT
N31     SBRT    star number by brightness rank (1st brightest ... 58th)
N32     SNMU    star name from its number
N33     TGET    almanac tables (TBL): Chebyshev lookup (not in NAVFULL_NOTBL)
