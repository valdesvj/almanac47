Almanac 47 v2.2.1 - QUICK START (C47 / R47; the DM42n with the C47 firmware)
==============================================================================

This version is for the SwissMicros C47 and R47 ONLY. It is written with the C47's structured
programming commands (IF ELSE ENDIF, DO WHILE ENDDO, REPEAT UNTIL: no GTO at all), which Free42,
Plus42 and the DM42 / HP-42S firmware do not have. Free42 / DM42 / Plus42 users (stock firmware):
Almanac47_Free42_DM42_v2.2.1.zip - the programs of v2.1.0, now keeping your stack (README_F42.txt).

FIRMWARE: a C47 / R47 firmware with ATEXT, GRFNT and the STRUCT commands: 00.109.05.00a0.ALPHA
(5 Oct 2026) or later. On the older public firmware 00.109.04.00b0 use the v1.1.0 release
(Almanac47_C47_R47_fw0400b0_v1.1.0.zip).

THE FILES (.p47 ready to load; the .txt are the same programs as listings)
  ALMA47.p47        the programs that stay on the calculator: the menu and every view
  NAVINIT_FAST.p47  builds the matrices, valid 2026-2030 (a fitted series: smaller and faster)
  NAVINIT_FULL.p47  builds the matrices, valid 2000-2050 (VSOP87 and Meeus: about twice the memory)
                    Both are the program NAVINIT: load ONE of them.
  MOON47.p47        the Moon phase on its own (needs nothing else)
  ALMA47_LABELS.txt the labels of ALMA47, and the routines you can call yourself
  p47u/             the same programs as rejig sources, with comments (for the C47 PROGRAMS folder)
  C47_Nav_User_Manual.pdf   the manual: every view, the labels, MOON47
The old DM42 with the C47 firmware (64 KiB): Almanac47_DM42_C47firmware_v2.2.1.zip - NAVLITTLE (Sun and
stars) with its reduced NAVINIT_LITTLE and a MOON47 of its own; needs a DM42 firmware package with STRUCT
(1, 2 or 3: at most 10 IF / DO / REPEAT a program); see its README_DM42.txt. The DM42n runs ALMA47. The almanac tables (TBL) of earlier versions are not part of v2.2.x.

1. LOAD   ALMA47.p47 and NAVINIT_FAST.p47 (or NAVINIT_FULL.p47): copy them to the calculator
          (USB disk) and load them (I/O menu, READP).
2. BUILD THE MATRICES (once)
          XEQ "NAVINIT"   takes a while; it ends with MATRICES READY: FAST 2026-2030
                          (or FULL 2000-2050). The matrices stay in memory.
3. DELETE NAVINIT        GTO "NAVINIT", then DELP (the matrices stay; frees its memory).
          Do not use CLPALL: it deletes ALMA47 too. Run NAVINIT again only for a new period.
4. RUN    XEQ "ALMA47": it asks (once) DATE, UTC, LAT, LON with INPUT (R/S keeps the value shown):
            DATE  in the calculator's CLK date format, e.g. 2026.0927 (YYYY.MMDD; D.MY: 27.092026,
                  M.DY: 9.272026) - the message line shows which
            UTC   HH.MMSS, e.g. 18.30
            LAT   DD.MMm, south negative, e.g. 25.12 = 25 deg 12 min N
            LON   DDD.MMm, west negative, e.g. 55.18 = 55 deg 18 min E
          The hourglass in the top bar and the SINKING....ABOUT box show while the sky is computed,
          then the menu:
            1 ALMANAC  2 SPLIT  3 SKY  4 ANIM  5 ALLSKY  6 INFO  0 END
            KEY A NUMBER   + MENU   up/down arrows +-1 HOUR   9 SNAP
          Press the number (no R/S). On a view: + back to the menu, up / down arrow the same view
          one hour later / earlier, 9 SNAP (a screenshot). On the menu the arrows change the hour too.
          On ALMANAC and SPLIT a key 1-8 picks that row of the table: ALMA47 ends with the body on
          your stack - Z its text ("SUN Zn 216.7 Hc 54 11.6"), Y Zn, X Hc (decimal degrees).
            1 ALMANAC  GHA, Dec, Hc, Zn of the Sun, the Moon, a planet, the brightest stars; twilight,
                       rise/set, meridian passage, the Moon (lit, phase, age, HP, SD)
            2 SPLIT    horizon chart on top, the bodies below (8 rows)
            3 SKY      horizon chart, the name of each body in turn next to it (2 s each)
            4 ANIM     the Sun and the Moon moving over 12 hours (24 frames, 1 s each)
            5 ALLSKY   the whole sky: over the horizon above, under the horizon below
            6 INFO     the repository, the licence, no warranty, cross-check with the almanac
          0 on the menu ends ALMA47. New date or position: XEQ "ALMA47" again; the same date, time
          and place show the views at once (the sky is kept in the matrix ALMC between runs).

REGISTERS AND STACK: ALMA47 gives you back your stack (X ... D, the 8 levels) and LASTx when it ends
  (0, or a key 1-8 with the body pushed on top). It saves them with LocR 9 / STOS R.00 (LASTx in R.08)
  and gives them back with RCLS R.00: the trick from the SwissMicros forum. It uses R07-R98 while it
  runs and clears only those (R-CLR 7.92): R00-R06 and R99 stay yours; your stack size comes back.
  Named variables and matrices are not touched (DATE, UTC, LAT, LON, the matrices).
  If you stop it with EXIT or R/S, the registers keep ALMA47's values: the routines of
  ALMA47_LABELS.txt (Sun, Moon, planets, Hc / Zn, sunrise ...) can then be called from the keyboard.

  An X in the top right corner: the date is outside the matrices' period (FAST: 2026-2030).
  A body below the horizon (in practice the Sun) has its Hc shown white on black.

MEMORY (program bytes, measured with rejig): ALMA47 27.2 KB, MOON47 4.5 KB; NAVINIT while loaded:
  FAST 28.5 KB, FULL 57.8 KB. The matrices: FAST about 55 KB, FULL about 95 KB; the cache ALMC about 6 KB.

WHAT IS NEW IN v2.2.1: your stack and LASTx kept (ALMA47, MOON47), only R07-R98 (MOON47
  R07-R65) used and cleared, the keys 1-8 on ALMANAC and SPLIT. The pages are those of v2.2.0.

WHAT WAS NEW IN v2.2.0: the same screens and results as the v2.1.0 views (with the new top bar and the
  hourglass), much faster: every view and every hour about 20-55 % fewer steps run than v2.1.0 (ALMANAC
  -18 %, SPLIT -42 %, SKY -53 %, ANIM -54 %, ALLSKY -40 %), and about 85 % less of the work the C47
  spends looking for labels and returns. Details: OPTIMIZATIONS.md.

MOON47: XEQ "MOON47". It asks nothing (the clock; if the clock is not on UT: 4 STO "TZ" for UT+4).
  +/- turns the picture to the view from the south, any other key ends. Your stack and LASTx come
  back; it uses R07-R65 and clears only those.

DOES NOT REPLACE THE NAUTICAL ALMANAC.
