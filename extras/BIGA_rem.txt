LBL "BIGA"
REM "BIGA - write a big letter A, then invert it ... and you get a bug (an ant)."
REM "Shows AGRAPH and the graphics mode GRMOD: 0 = OR (draw), 3 = XOR (invert)."
REM "The A is the 5x7 letter made 8 times bigger: 40 pixels wide, 56 high."
REM "Registers: R30 = column (x), R31 = row (y), R32 = column pattern, R33 = counter."
REM "Screen: 400 x 240 pixels, (0,0) = bottom left."
REM "--- 1. Prepare ---"
REM "WSIZE 63: binary numbers up to 62 bits (one AGRAPH = one column, up to 62 pixels)"
WSIZE 63
CLLCD
REM "Mode 0 = OR: AGRAPH only switches pixels on"
0
STO 00
GRMOD 00
REM "Bottom left corner of the A: column 180, row 92 (the middle of the screen)"
92
STO 31
180
STO 30
REM "--- 2. Write the A (5 columns of the small A, each one 8 pixels wide) ---"
REM "Left leg: 48 pixels on (rows 0-47), the top 8 rows off"
111111111111111111111111111111111111111111111111#2
STO 32
8
XEQ 10
REM "Middle: top bar (rows 48-55) and crossbar (rows 24-31), 3 x 8 = 24 columns"
11111111000000000000000011111111000000000000000000000000#2
STO 32
24
XEQ 10
REM "Right leg: the same as the left leg"
111111111111111111111111111111111111111111111111#2
STO 32
8
XEQ 10
REM "Show the A for 1 second"
PAUSE 10
REM "--- 3. Invert the A: the bug ---"
REM "Mode 3 = XOR: AGRAPH switches every pixel it touches (on -> off, off -> on)"
3
STO 03
GRMOD 03
REM "Draw a solid block exactly over the A (40 columns of 56 pixels, all on)"
REM "The A disappears and its empty space turns black: head, body and feelers"
180
STO 30
11111111111111111111111111111111111111111111111111111111#2
STO 32
40
XEQ 10
REM "--- 4. Tidy up and show the bug ---"
REM "Back to mode 0 (OR) and the normal word size"
0
STO 00
GRMOD 00
WSIZE 64
REM "Keep the bug on the screen for 10 seconds"
PAUSE 99
RTN
REM "--- LBL 10: draw the pattern in R32 as X columns side by side ---"
REM "One AGRAPH: Y = row (R31), X = column (R30), pattern in R32; bit 0 = bottom pixel"
LBL 10
STO 33
LBL 11
RCL 31
RCL 30
AGRAPH 32
REM "Next column to the right"
1
STO+ 30
DSE 33
GTO 11
RTN
END
