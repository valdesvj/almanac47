---- What it is ----
Almanac 47 is a celestial navigation almanac for the C47 / R47. For a date, a time (UT) and your dead-reckoning
position it gives the Sun, the Moon, the planets and 58 navigation stars: GHA, declination, the computed altitude
Hc and the azimuth Zn, ready for a sight reduction; sunrise, sunset and twilight, meridian passage, the Moon's
phase, and charts of the sky: the horizon, the whole sky, the Sun and Moon moving over twelve hours.
It does not replace the Nautical Almanac: cross-check before you navigate with it.

---- How to use it ----
You need two files: ALMA47 (the programs that stay on the calculator) and NAVINIT (builds the data once).
  1. Load ALMA47 and NAVINIT (NAVINIT.p47u: valid 2026-2030, or NAVINIT_FULL.p47u: 2000-2050, more memory).
  2. XEQ NAVINIT once. It takes a while and ends with MATRICES READY. Then delete NAVINIT (GTO NAVINIT, DELP):
     the data stays. Do not use CLPALL, it deletes ALMA47 too.
  3. XEQ ALMA47. It asks four values (R/S keeps the value shown):
       DATE  as the calculator's date format, e.g. 2026.0927        UTC  HH.MMSS, e.g. 18.30
       LAT   DD.MM, south negative, e.g. 25.12 = 25 12' N           LON  DDD.MM, west negative
  4. The menu: press a number (no R/S).
       1 ALMANAC  the table of the bodies: GHA, Dec, Hc, Zn; twilight, rise / set, meridian passage, the Moon
       2 SPLIT    a horizon chart above, the bodies below
       3 SKY      the horizon chart, the name of each body in turn
       4 ANIM     the Sun and the Moon moving over 12 hours
       5 ALLSKY   the whole sky, above and below the horizon
       6 INFO     about the program;   9 SNAP a picture of the screen;   0 the end
     In a view: + back to the menu, the up / down arrows the same view one hour later / earlier.
     On ALMANAC and SPLIT a key 1-8 picks that row: ALMA47 ends with the body on your stack:
     Z its text (SUN Zn 216.7 Hc 54 11.6), Y its Zn, X its Hc in decimal degrees.
  An X in the top right corner means the date is outside the data's period. A body below the horizon has its
  Hc shown white on black. The same date, time and place again show the views at once (the sky is kept).

---- What it changes on your calculator ----
Your stack (all 8 levels) and LASTx are given back when it ends (saved with LocR 9 / STOS, the trick from the
SwissMicros forum). It uses the registers R07-R98 and clears only those: R00-R06 and R99 stay yours. It keeps its
named matrices (the data from NAVINIT and the cache ALMC) and the variables DATE, UTC, LAT, LON between runs, and
uses some named work variables while it runs. Memory: ALMA47 about 27 KB; the data about 55 KB (FAST) or 95 KB (FULL).

---- Programs and labels ----
ALMA47 is one file of 27 programs: the menu ALMA47 and 26 routines named N01 ... N98. Inside each program every
routine is a local label; the N names are global only because a C47 program can hold at most 100 local labels and
Almanac 47 has about 370 (the N-numbers are listed in ALMA47_LABELS.txt of the release: you can call the Sun,
Moon, planet and sight-reduction routines yourself after leaving with EXIT). Written with the STRUCT commands
(IF / ELSE / ENDIF, DO / WHILE / ENDDO, REPEAT / UNTIL, no GTO), ATEXT, GRFNT, AGRAPH, PIXEL and the matrix
functions; it needs a C47 / R47 firmware with them (00.109.05.00a0.ALPHA of 5 Oct 2026 or later).
