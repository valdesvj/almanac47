---- What it is ----
MOON47 shows the Moon's phase for now, from the calculator's clock: the Moon drawn as it looks, the part lit (%),
its age in days, its horizontal parallax HP and semi-diameter SD (for sextant work), and the dates and times (UT)
of the next new Moon, first quarter, full Moon and last quarter with their symbols. It uses 20 terms of Meeus'
Astronomical Algorithms ch. 47: the phases within about 4 minutes, HP within 0.03'.

---- How to use it ----
Load MOON47 and press XEQ MOON47. It asks nothing: it reads the date and time from the clock as UT. If your clock
is on local time, tell it the zone once: 4 STO "TZ" for a clock on UT+4 (TZ stays for the next runs).
  +/-        turns the picture to the view from the southern hemisphere
  any other key ends
Example: clock 03-10-2026 12:00 UT: LAST QUARTER, 51 % lit, age 22.4 days, HP 59.2', SD 16.1'.

---- What it changes on your calculator ----
Your stack (all 8 levels) and LASTx are given back at the end (LocR 9 / STOS, the trick from the SwissMicros
forum). It uses the registers R07-R65 and clears only those: R00-R06 and R66-R99 stay yours. Its work matrices
M7T, M7C, M7A, M7V, M7W stay (M7T and M7C set to 0 at the end); the variable TZ stays if you made it.
It needs no other program and no NAVINIT.

---- Programs and labels ----
One program, MOON47, with one global label (MOON47): the text printers and the phase symbols are local labels in
it. Written with the STRUCT commands (IF / ELSE / ENDIF, DO / WHILE / ENDDO, REPEAT / UNTIL, no GTO) and ATEXT;
it needs a C47 / R47 firmware with them (00.109.05.00a0.ALPHA of 5 Oct 2026 or later).
