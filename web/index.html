Almanac 47 celestial navigation · Zones 47

Almanac 47 and Zones 47 by Victor Valdes, Master Mariner · github.com/valdesvj/almanac47 · github.com/valdesvj/zones47 · github.com/valdesvj
The same methods as the C47 / R47 programs: VSOP87D (truncated) and IAU 1980 nutation for the Sun, Hipparcos J2000 stars with precession, nutation and aberration, Meeus for the Moon; time zones from the IANA tz database. Time = UT1 (UTC + DUT1, within 0.9 s). Not for navigation: check against the Nautical Almanac.
